
# Parachute 🪂 <p style="font-size: 14px;">Autonomous on-chain crypto crash insurance, powered by Somnia Agent</p>

### The Problem <br> <br><p style = "font-size: 16px"> Crypto markets crash fast. By the time you react, the damage is done. Existing insurance products require manual claims, human approval, and days of waiting defeating the purpose entirely.</p>

### What Parachute Does

<p>Parachute lets users set a price floor for any crypto asset and get paid out automatically the moment the market crashes through it, no claims form, no waiting, no human in the loop.</p>

<ol> <li> Set your floor — choose your asset, trigger price, and coverage amount</li>

<li> Pay your premium — small upfront cost locked into the pool</li>
<li> Forget about it — a Somnia Agent watches the price every hour, 24/7</li>
<li> Get paid — the moment the price breaches your floor, the contract pays you instantly</li>
</ol>

#### Why It Matters
<p>Parametric insurance — automatic payouts based on real-world data — is a proven $multi-billion market. </p>
<p>Parachute is the first implementation powered entirely by an on-chain AI agent, removing every point of human trust and delay from the process.</p>
